Brimtune

Your notch, playing along. Thanks for trying Brimtune! This page walks you through installing it, opening it the first time, and the permissions it asks for. It takes about a minute.

Needs: macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon or Intel. A notch is not required: on Macs and displays without one, a small island floats at the top of the screen.

1. Install

  1. Drag Brimtune onto the Applications folder in this window.
  2. Eject this disk (the ⏏ button next to “Brimtune” in Finder's sidebar). You can delete the downloaded Brimtune.dmg afterwards.
  3. Open Brimtune from Applications or Launchpad. Always run it from Applications, not from this window: otherwise updates and permissions won't stick.

2. The first time you open it

Brimtune is free and is signed by its maker rather than through Apple's paid developer programme, so the first time macOS shows a warning such as “Apple could not verify ‘Brimtune’ is free of malware” or “Brimtune can't be opened”. This happens once:

  1. Click Done (or OK) in that message.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down to Security. Next to “Brimtune” was blocked to protect your Mac, click Open Anyway.
  4. Confirm with your password or Touch ID, then click Open Anyway once more.

From then on Brimtune opens like any other app, and updates keep working without this step.

If macOS says Brimtune “is damaged and can't be opened”, the download was quarantined more strictly than usual. Open Terminal and run:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Brimtune.app

Then open Brimtune again.

3. Permissions

A setup guide opens on first launch and asks for each permission in turn, explaining what it's for. Only the first group is needed for music; everything else is optional and can be changed later in Brimtune's Settings or in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

For your music

  • Music and Spotify apps. The first time they play, macOS asks whether Brimtune may control them. Click Allow. This lets Brimtune see what's playing and send play, pause and skip. (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation.)
  • Safari or Chrome, for YouTube Music and Spotify on the web. Allow Brimtune to control your browser when asked (also under Automation).
  • Covers, progress, Up Next and playlists from the browser need one switch in the browser itself:
    • Safari: Safari → Settings → Advanced → turn on Show features for web developers. Then open the new Developer tab and turn on Allow JavaScript from Apple Events.
    • Chrome: View → Developer → Allow JavaScript from Apple Events.

Without that switch Brimtune still shows the song's title and finds a cover by itself, and the controls keep working.

Optional extras

  • Waveform that follows the music: System Audio Recording (Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording). Brimtune only measures loudness in memory. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • Upcoming meetings: Calendars. Shows a countdown five minutes before a meeting, with a Join button.
  • AirPods battery: Bluetooth. Shows the battery level when your headphones connect.
  • Open at login: adds Brimtune to Login Items so it starts with your Mac.

If you clicked Don't Allow by mistake, turn the permission on in System Settings → Privacy & Security, then quit and reopen Brimtune. You can reopen the setup guide at any time from the menu bar icon → Setup Guide.

4. Using it

  • Brimtune has no Dock icon. It lives in the notch and in the menu bar (a tiny island icon).
  • Hover the notch to open the player, search and your playlists. Click the cover for the full-screen player with lyrics.
  • Lock your Mac to see the player and lyrics on the lock screen.
  • Timers, the stopwatch, your listening history, Settings and Check for Updates are in the menu bar icon.

5. Something not working?

  • Nothing appears in the notch: play something first; the island stays quiet when nothing is playing. Check that Brimtune is running (its menu bar icon is there).
  • A browser tab isn't picked up: Brimtune follows music.youtube.com and open.spotify.com in Safari and Chrome. Check the Automation permission and the Allow JavaScript from Apple Events switch above.
  • No lyrics: not every song has synced lyrics. If they run early or late, click the line being sung in the full-screen player.
  • Permissions stopped working after moving the app: keep Brimtune in Applications, then quit and reopen it.

Updates and removal

Updates download on their own and keep your permissions; you can also check from the menu bar icon. To remove Brimtune, choose Quit Brimtune from its menu bar icon and drag it from Applications to the Bin. To remove its history too, delete the folder Library/Application Support/Brimtune in your home folder.

Privacy and help
