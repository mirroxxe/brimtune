07 Install

Install in a minute.

  1. Download Brimtune and open the file.
  2. Drag Brimtune into Applications in the window that appears.
  3. Open it. The first time, macOS may say it can't verify the developer: Brimtune is free and signed by its maker rather than through Apple's paid programme. Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You do this once.
  4. Follow the setup guide. It asks for each permission and explains what it's for. Listening in Safari or Chrome? Turn on Allow JavaScript from Apple Events in that browser when the guide asks: it's the one step not to skip, and the guide checks it for you. Every other extra is optional.

08 Questions

Before you ask.

Will it slow down my Mac or drain the battery?

No. It waits for something to happen instead of constantly checking. With music paused it uses around 1.5% of one processor core and 36 MB of memory.

Does my Mac need a notch?

No. On Macs and external displays without one, Brimtune shows a small floating island at the top centre of the screen, and only when something's playing.

Which music apps work?

The Apple Music and Spotify apps, and YouTube Music or Spotify in Safari or Chrome. For covers, progress, Up Next and your playlists from the browser, turn on Allow JavaScript from Apple Events in that browser: in Safari it's under Settings, Advanced, "Show features for web developers", then the Developer tab; in Chrome it's View, Developer. The setup guide shows you where and checks it's on.

Why does macOS warn me the first time I open it?

Apple only skips that warning for developers in its paid programme. Brimtune is signed by its maker, so macOS asks you to confirm once in Privacy & Security. After that it opens like any other app, and updates keep your permissions.

What does it do with my data?

Nothing leaves your Mac except the song details needed to find lyrics and covers. There's no account and no tracking. The privacy policy lists exactly what goes where.

Is Brimtune open source?

Yes. The code is on GitHub under the GPL-3.0 licence: read it, build it yourself, report a bug or send a fix.

How do I remove it?

Choose Quit Brimtune from its menu bar icon, then drag Brimtune from Applications to the Bin. To remove its history too, delete the folder Library/Application Support/Brimtune in your home folder.
